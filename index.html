Who calls us

One contractor.Four kinds of jobs.

We're general contractors, excavators, home builders, and plumbers. That means one call covers the site, the utilities, and the build.

Builders & GCs

Pads graded to plan, footings dug, utilities stubbed. Your framers show up to a site that's ready.

Developers

Lot clearing, road cuts, and water and sewer runs for subdivisions and commercial sites.

Homeowners

Building on your own land? We clear it, grade it, run the lines, and can build the house too.

Farms & land

Driveways, drainage, pads for barns and shops, and clearing that turns woods into usable ground.

How we grade a site

01 Survey & stake

We walk the site with your plans and set grade stakes.

02 Clear & grub

Trees, stumps, and topsoil come off and get stockpiled or hauled.

03 Cut & fill

High ground fills the low ground. Less dirt hauled, less cost.

04 Compact & grade

Fill goes down in lifts and gets compacted, then fine graded.

05 Utilities & pad

Water and sewer lines in, pad ready for footings or the build.
